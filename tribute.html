Sushant Singh Rajput

Indian Actor

Sushant Singh Rajput was widely described as a deeply intelligent,intellectually curious sensitive individual who was much more than a conventional film star. He was a brilliant student and He won an National Olympiad in physics , He will be forever remembered in our hearts as an kind person and best actor.
